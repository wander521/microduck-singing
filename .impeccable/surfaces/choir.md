# Quartet extension

## Direction contract

THESIS: Four seated MicroDucks sing four individually selectable pieces. The familiar full-screen arcade composition puts the quartet before the controls.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit cream enamel, marine ink, coral action, Bungee DUCK! mark and rounded docks from MicroDuck Arcade. Warm daylight reaches a circular wooden rehearsal floor. Lilac, graphite, sky and ivory shells echo the reference video.

STORY: Choose a piece, press play, watch four distinct voices move their beaks and heads together; pause, replay, or isolate one part.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Brand at top left, four-piece programme at left, playback state top right, quartet shown in close-up at centre, voice audition below the stage and transport along the bottom. Mobile stacks the programme below a large unobstructed quartet; a wider field of view keeps all four ducks visible.

FORM: Code-led scoped extension of the existing full-screen arcade layout, inherited seed5069bd2a. Signature interaction is each voice's written vowel and note driving its own beak and head on the audio clock. User confirmed four songs.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
