Reused original scores: sounds/scores/wistful.duckscore and duck_strut.mid.
Notes, written dynamics, vowels and tie merging retained in generated data.
This standalone adaptation uses the original 58/126 BPM score tempi.
Outer Wilds test material is not included.

Adapted voice harmonic/formant logic and head_expression from official music
and robotd sources. Browser personalities, Web Audio graph, transport, visual
seated quartet, Moon Quack March and Ode to Joy arrangement are additions by
this project. This is not bit-identical Rust Stream, Bluetooth synchronization,
physical simulation, trained audio RL or hardware validation.
